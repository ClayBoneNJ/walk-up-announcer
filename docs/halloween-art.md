# Halloween artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool. The project asset is
`v2/public/assets/halloween-haunted-ballpark.webp` (1536 × 1024, about 214 KB).
The generated PNG was encoded to WebP for mobile/offline loading.

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create a polished cinematic illustrated website background for a youth baseball app's spooky Halloween edition. Landscape 1536x1024 composition. A haunted baseball ballpark at midnight, glowing carved jack-o-lanterns with baseball stitching carved into a few pumpkins in the foreground lower corners, old crooked wooden outfield fence, swirling violet ground fog across a baseball diamond, distant dark grandstands, bare twisted trees framing the left and right edges, flying bats silhouetted across a huge warm ivory full moon in the upper right. Palette deep ink black, aubergine purple, vivid burnt orange pumpkin glow, small eerie acid green accents. Rich painterly game-poster detail, atmospheric theatrical lighting, playful spooky for kids, no gore, no people, no lettering, no text, no logos, no watermark. Keep the upper left and central 55 percent dark and low-detail so interface text remains legible; concentrate the moon and larger pumpkin details toward right edge and lower foreground. Full bleed premium Halloween illustration; not an interface mockup.

The pumpkin jersey badge is a local SVG. Creepster is bundled with its OFL license
in `v2/public/assets/fonts/`; both the artwork and font are cached for offline use.
